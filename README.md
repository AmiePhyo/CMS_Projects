# QuickBite Delivery 

## Project Overview
QuickBite Delivery is a responsive online food delivery website that allows customers to browse restaurants, view menus, add food items to a shopping cart, manage quantities, and place orders online.

The website is designed to provide a simple and user-friendly experience for customers who want to order food from multiple restaurants quickly and conveniently.

---

## Features

### Home Page
- Attractive hero section
- Introduction to QuickBite Delivery
- Featured restaurants and food items
- Navigation to all website pages

### Restaurants Page
- Display available restaurant partners
- Restaurant images and descriptions
- View restaurant menus

### Menu Page
- Browse food items
- Food names, images, and prices
- Add food items to cart
- Increase or decrease quantity before adding

### Shopping Cart
- View selected food items
- Update item quantities
- Remove items from cart
- Automatic total price calculation
- Cart item notification badge in navigation bar

### Checkout Page
- Customer information form
- Delivery details
- Payment method selection
- Order summary

### Success Page
- Order confirmation message
- Successful order notification

### 📞 Contact Page
- Contact form
- Business information
- Customer support details
